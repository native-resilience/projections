Native Resilience · A service of the Montana Climate Office

What this is

Native Resilience partners at the Montana Climate Office have extracted place-based climate data for Native American, Alaska Native, and Native Hawaiian lands located in the United States. Climate data and projections for temperature, precipitation, and other metrics related to crop, livestock and forestry agriculture are shown in a PDF report for each land, with the underlying data available as a download.

The data derive from eight Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6 (CMIP6) global climate models and four socioeconomic scenarios for the period 2015 to 2100, plus each model's historical simulation for 1950 to 2014. Raw data are extracted for the location of each land from the NASA Earth Exchange Global Daily Downscaled Projections dataset (NEX-GDDP-CMIP6).
